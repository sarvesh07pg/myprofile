Our Story

Shirpur's scoop shop, done the natural way

Natural Icecreams opened on Karwand Naka with one simple idea: ice cream should taste like the fruit it's named after. Mango should taste like mango. Strawberry should taste like strawberry. That means sourcing fruit by season, churning in small batches, and never reaching for a shortcut jar of flavouring.

Walk in on a hot Shirpur afternoon and you'll find neighbours, families, and regulars who've been coming since we opened — for a scoop, a shake, or just to cool off with people they know.

A few favourites

Our lineup changes with the season — here's what regulars keep coming back for.

Alphonso Mango

Made with real Alphonso pulp when it's in season — rich, golden, and unmistakably mango.

Fresh Strawberry

Whole strawberries folded into cream, with just enough tang to cut the sweetness.

Kesar Pista

Saffron-steeped milk and roasted pistachio — a classic done properly, slow and fragrant.

Tender Coconut

Fresh coconut water and malai, scooped straight from the shell flavour you know.

Belgian Chocolate

Dark, slow-melted chocolate for the purists who just want chocolate to taste like chocolate.

Butterscotch Crunch

Caramelised sugar with a proper crunch folded through every bite, not just on top.

Anjeer Fig

Malai Classic

Our plainest scoop and our best seller — pure, creamy, and endlessly comforting.
